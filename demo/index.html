A scrollable region

Scrollbar width, thumb, and track follow the active theme.

  1. Use one token source for colors and material.
  2. Choose an action variant by its meaning.
  3. Keep icon buttons circular and text buttons pill shaped.
  4. Place labels next to the controls they describe.
  5. Use real form values and events.
  6. Keep keyboard focus visible.
  7. Honor reduced motion preferences.
  8. Align every table cell vertically.
  9. Keep popovers inside the viewport.
  10. Test both materials and both themes.
  11. Retain third-party license notices.
  12. Promote reusable rules into the framework.
